  1. Choose appliances by clicking their cards. Click again to remove one.

  3. Watch the readings. Each appliance shows its watts; the overview adds them all together.

What do the numbers mean?

W (watts) is power used right now. A higher number means more electricity is being used at that moment.

kWh (kilowatt-hours) is energy added up over time. For example, 1,000 W used for one hour equals 1 kWh.

These are simulated readings. The total includes a preset starting value, so it does not begin at zero. The optional sections below are for exploring problems and technical details.
